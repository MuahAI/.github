# Muah AI

A browser companion you can open, shape and return to. Ready characters or your own. Chat stays in one thread. Images and voice unlock on paid plans. Free start is short. Check the live site for current limits and any promo.

## What you get

Open a character or build one with a name, look and short background. Send messages. Stay in the same chat if you want earlier details to come back. Pictures of the character and voice replies sit on paid plans. The free tier is enough to see the tone before you decide.

Most people try one ready character first. If the replies fit, they stay or make their own. If not, they leave. No long setup required.

---

## How it usually goes

1. Open the site and pick a character or start the builder.  
2. Send a few messages and see if the tone matches.  
3. Stay in that thread if you want continuity.  
4. Upgrade later if you want more messages, images or voice.

Memory is better on paid plans. Free tiers forget sooner. Chips or similar meters are common for pictures and voice. They run out.

## What to check first

- How many free messages you get. A handful is not a daily allowance.  
- What the paid plan actually unlocks.  
- Whether you can cancel from the account page.  
- Do not put real names, phone numbers or other personal details in the chat.

A few days of free use shows if the character holds the conversation. After that the choice is about media and how long you want to stay.

---

## Who it fits

- You want a character you can open and talk to without a long setup.  
- You prefer one thread you can return to.  
- You are fine testing free before paying.  
- You may want images or voice later.

It is less useful if you only need a general chatbot with no character. The product is built around companions.
